⚘ Lineage — User Guide
Welcome to Lineage

Lineage lets you record your family history across up to 10 generations — names, photos, professions, education, and personal stories. Everything is saved on your own device and works without internet.

1. Adding a family member

Click the Add Member button (top right). Fill in the details across the four tabs:

  • Personal — name, gender, birth/death dates, birthplace, religion, language.
  • Family — choose the generation (G1 = oldest ancestor, G10 = youngest), relationship, parents, spouse, children.
  • Career — profession, employer, education, hobbies, achievements.
  • Photo & Story — upload one photo and write their personal story.

Click Save Member when done. Only the first name is required — fill in as much or as little as you like.

2. Choosing the generation

The generation number decides where the person appears in the tree. Use the coloured G1–G10 buttons in the Family tab. Generation 1 is your oldest known ancestor; each later generation is their children, grandchildren, and so on down to Generation 10.

3. Viewing your tree
  • Tree view — shows all members in colour-coded generation rows, oldest at top.
  • Cards view — a grid of cards for quick browsing.
  • Search box — find anyone by name, profession, or place.
  • Generation dots — click a coloured dot to show only that generation; click again to clear.
4. Editing or deleting

Click any member to open their profile on the right. Use the Edit button to change their details, or the Delete (bin) button to remove them. Deleting one member never affects anyone else.

5. Backup — keep your data safe

Your data is stored in this browser. To keep it safe, click Backup regularly. This saves a file with all your family records. Store it in Google Drive, email, or a USB drive. If you ever lose your data or switch devices, use Import & Merge to restore it.

⚠️ Always take a backup before clearing your browser or switching to a new phone or computer.

6. Sharing with relatives (Smart Merge)

If a relative also uses Lineage, they can send you their backup file. Click Import & Merge and select their file. The Smart Merge Wizard finds people who appear in both trees and lets you choose which version to keep, or combine both — so no one is duplicated and no data is lost.

7. Upgrading your plan (Trial → Annual / Lifetime)

Started with the Trial and want to upgrade to Annual or Lifetime? It’s simple, and you will NOT lose any of your family data.

Your family tree is saved safely in this browser — it is completely separate from your licence key. So changing your key never touches your data.

To upgrade:

  • Buy the Annual or Lifetime plan at anilksinha.github.io — you’ll get a new licence key.
  • Come back here and click the button below.
  • Enter your new key and activate. That’s it — your whole family tree stays exactly as it was.

✓ Tip: take a Backup first, just for peace of mind. But your data will not be lost.

Tips
  • Approximate dates like "c. 1905" are perfectly fine.
  • Separate hobbies with commas — each becomes a tag.
  • Compress large photos before uploading to save space.
  • Write detailed stories — future generations will treasure them.
